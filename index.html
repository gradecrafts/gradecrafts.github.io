A few useful answers

Good questions start here.

Which subjects can I ask about?

Grade Crafts offers tutoring in Maths, Science, English Language, Reading, Economics and Accounting, from primary school through university level. Share your current level, course, and learning goals when enquiring.

Can schools enquire about support?

Yes. Schools can enquire about intervention sessions and educational consultancy. Tell us about the subjects, pupil groups, and priorities you have in mind.

How do I find out about prices and availability?

These details will be confirmed before booking. Email us to discuss your needs. Booking and payment arrangements will be confirmed directly.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both 1:1 and group tutoring are available online and face to face, from primary school through university level. Face-to-face locations, availability, and specific courses supported will be confirmed when you enquire.
