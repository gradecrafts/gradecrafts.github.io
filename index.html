A few useful answers

Good questions start here.

Which subjects can I ask about?

Our subjects include Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Business Studies, Economics, Accounts, Science, English, literacy and reading. We also support SATs and 11+ preparation. See each tutor’s profile for the specific subjects and levels offered.

What happens after I request a consultation?

A Grade Crafts consultant or tutor will contact you to discuss your needs and arrange a suitable time. Session options, availability and prices will be confirmed directly.

Are your tutors DBS checked?

All Grade Crafts tutors are DBS checked. We follow safer recruitment policies and procedures.

Can schools enquire about support?

Yes. Schools can enquire about GCSE booster classes, interventions, specialist consultancy and school improvement plan support. Tell us about your pupil groups and priorities.

How do I find out about prices and availability?

These details will be confirmed before booking. Email us to discuss your needs. Booking and payment arrangements will be confirmed directly.

Are sessions online or in person?

We offer one-to-one and group sessions online and face to face. Face-to-face tuition serves London and the surrounding areas. Online learning platforms and revision resources also support learning between sessions. Enquire about availability and a suitable location.
